Fully custom. We start with an audit of your agency's workflow and build systems around how you actually operate — not a one-size-fits-all template.

A focused first system is typically live in about 2 weeks. Larger multi-workflow systems run 3–6 weeks. You see time savings starting week one of launch.

Do I need technical knowledge?

None. We build, deploy, document, and train your team. You use the outputs — ranked shortlists, clean CRM, sent follow-ups — not the plumbing.

What tools do you integrate?

n8n, Airtable, Notion, Slack, OpenAI, Claude, Gmail, and most major recruitment CRMs (Bullhorn, JobAdder, Vincere, HubSpot and more). If it has an API, we can connect it.

Engagements start at a one-time build plus an optional monthly retainer for maintenance and new automations. We scope and quote on the strategy call based on your size and goals.
